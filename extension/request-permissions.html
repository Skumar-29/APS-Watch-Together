Allow camera and microphone

Chrome cannot show this permission prompt inside the side panel on some Macs. Grant it once in this full tab, then your private room will continue automatically.

Your privacy Your camera and microphone are used only for the peer-to-peer call. Movie video is never captured or sent through APS servers.

After clicking, choose Allow in Chrome’s prompt.

Permission still blocked?

Open macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and Microphone, then enable Google Chrome. Fully quit and reopen Chrome afterward.
