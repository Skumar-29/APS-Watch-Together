APS WATCH TOGETHER V1.3.0 — FRIEND INSTALLATION

1. Save APS-Watch-Together-Extension.zip to Documents.
2. Double-click the ZIP to extract it.
3. Open Google Chrome.
4. Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
5. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
6. Click Load unpacked.
7. Select the extracted APS-Watch-Together-Extension folder containing manifest.json.
8. Pin APS Watch Together from Chrome's puzzle-piece Extensions menu.
9. Open Netflix, Prime Video or ZEE5 in Chrome.
10. Open APS Watch Together, enter your name and choose one mode:
    - Video + Audio
    - Audio only
    - Video only
    - Watch only (no camera or microphone required)
11. Enter the room code and click Join.
12. Grant only the camera/microphone permissions required by the selected mode.
13. Open the same movie or episode. Click Play once if Chrome requests player interaction.

Important:
- Watch-only users can still follow host playback, chat, react, see friends' cameras and hear friends.
- Use headphones to prevent echo.
- Keep the extracted extension folder; do not move or delete it while installed.
- Every participant uses their own streaming-service account.
- APS never transmits the movie; it synchronizes playback commands only.
