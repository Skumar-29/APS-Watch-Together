APS WATCH TOGETHER V1.5.1 — FRIEND INSTALLATION

WHAT YOUR FRIEND RECEIVES
APS-Watch-Together-Extension-v1.5.1-Collaboration-Update.zip

INSTALL
1. Save the ZIP in Documents.
2. Extract it and keep the extracted folder permanently.
3. Open Google Chrome.
4. Enter chrome://extensions in the address bar.
5. Turn on Developer mode.
6. Click Load unpacked.
7. Select the extracted APS-Watch-Together-Extension folder that directly contains manifest.json.
8. Confirm Chrome shows APS Watch Together version 1.5.1.
9. Pin APS from Chrome's puzzle-piece menu.
10. Refresh any Netflix, Prime Video or ZEE5 tab that was already open.

JOIN BY DIRECT LINK
1. Click the invitation link sent by the host.
2. On the APS invitation page, click Open APS and join room.
3. Enter your name if required.
4. APS opens with the room code already filled in and joins when possible.
5. Open the same movie/episode in Chrome using your own account.

JOIN BY ROOM CODE
1. Open the same movie/episode in Chrome.
2. Open APS Watch Together.
3. Enter your name and the eight-character room code.
4. Choose Camera and Microphone on/off.
5. Click Join.

DURING THE ROOM
- Mic: turn the microphone off/on or attach a newly connected mic.
- Camera: turn the camera off/on or attach a newly connected camera.
- Share: select a normal Chrome tab, application window or display.
- Devices: switch camera, microphone, speaker/headphones or quality without leaving.
- Cinema: hide full controls and use the compact/floating call view.
- Camera previews remain organized automatically; Me and Friends can be hidden independently in Cinema Mode.

IMPORTANT
- Use headphones to avoid echo.
- Only one person shares a screen at a time.
- Chrome always asks the presenter to choose what to share.
- Protected OTT movie video may appear black or stop when captured; APS does not bypass DRM.
- Everyone watches the movie through their own streaming account.
- Do not move or delete the extracted extension folder after installation.
