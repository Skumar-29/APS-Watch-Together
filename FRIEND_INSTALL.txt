APS WATCH TOGETHER — FRIEND INSTALLATION

1. Save the supplied APS-Watch-Together-Extension.zip file to Documents.
2. Double-click the ZIP to extract it.
3. Open Google Chrome.
4. Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
5. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
6. Click Load unpacked.
7. Select the extracted APS-Watch-Together-Extension folder.
8. Click Chrome's puzzle-piece Extensions icon and pin APS Watch Together.
9. Open Netflix, Prime Video or ZEE5 in Chrome.
10. Click APS Watch Together, enter your name and join the room code your host sends.
11. Allow camera and microphone access.
12. Open the same movie/episode. Click Play once if Chrome asks for player interaction.

Important:
- Keep the extracted folder. Do not delete or move it while the extension is installed.
- Use your own streaming-service account.
- The movie is never sent through APS; only playback commands are synchronized.
