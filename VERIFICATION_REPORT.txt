Expected Cinema Mode workflow
-----------------------------
1. Join or create a room in the full side panel.
2. Click Cinema.
3. Cinema Mode joins the same room and preserves host ownership.
4. The full side panel closes automatically.
5. Friend camera streams appear in the compact window.
6. Use Me and Friends independently to hide or show either camera group.
7. Click Float call for an always-on-top movable view with the same toggles.
8. Click Full controls to restore the side panel safely.
